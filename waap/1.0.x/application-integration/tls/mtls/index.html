Client Certificate Validation (mTLS)

Normally only Aero WAAP proves its identity to the client, by presenting the virtual host's server certificate. With client certificate validation the connecting client has to prove its identity too: Aero WAAP asks for a certificate and rejects the connection unless it was issued by a CA you trust.

This is the client leg of the connection. For the certificate Aero WAAP presents when it connects onwards to your application server, see Backend TLS connections . The two are independent and easy to confuse.

Important

The setting lives on the listener, so it applies to every virtual host that shares that listener. You cannot require a client certificate for one application and leave another one open if both sit on the same listener. Give them separate listeners when their requirements differ.

Require a certificate from the client

  1. Open the listener and go to its TLS tab.
  2. Turn on "Client Certificate Validation (mTLS)".
  3. Select a Trusted CA Certificate . The selected CA must match the CA that will issue client certificates. Upload it beforehand if it is not in the list yet.
  4. Decide whether a certificate is mandatory. Leave "Optional validation" off to require one or turn it on to keep accepting connections that present none, which is useful while clients are still being rolled out.
  5. Turn on "Forward Client Certificate information with XFCC HTTP Header" if the application behind Aero WAAP needs to know which client it was.
  6. Create a revision and deploy it .

Accept only specific certificates

Trusting a CA means accepting every certificate that CA has issued. Where that is too broad, add a SAN Match to narrow it to particular identities:

  1. On the same tab, add a SAN Match.
  2. Choose the Type: Email matches the certificate's email SAN, Other Names matches another SAN entry and additionally needs an OID identifying which one.
  3. Choose the Matcher: Exact, Prefix, Suffix or Regex and enter the Value to compare against.

Hint

Requiring a certificate with no SAN Match accepts any client the CA has ever issued a certificate to.
