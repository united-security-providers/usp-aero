# Remove a component

> [!CAUTION]
> Removing a component will delete the entire configuration history of the component.
> This action can not be undone without a backup!

1. Open [Components](../../reference/gui/components.md).
2. Click on the trash icon ("Remove") of the designated component.
3. Confirm the removal by clicking "Remove".

The removal will take some minutes and when finished, the icon in the module switcher will disappear.
All the related services will be stopped.
